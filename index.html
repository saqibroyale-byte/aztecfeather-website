Our story

From Indian exhibitions to Dubai brunches

Aztec Feather started in India, selling handmade boho bags at exhibitions and online. Shoppers loved the beadwork, the coins and the fringe, and the community grew to more than 4,500 people on Instagram.

Now we're based in Dubai. We still work with the same artisan suppliers, and we bring in small batches of each design. Because every bag is made by hand, the beads and colours vary slightly from piece to piece.

Caring for your bag: spot clean gently with a dry or slightly damp cloth. Don't wash or soak it, and store it flat.
