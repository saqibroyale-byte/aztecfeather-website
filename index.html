Ask a question
Where do you deliver?

Anywhere in the UAE, sent from Dubai, for a flat AED 15. We confirm the delivery time on WhatsApp once your order is in. For orders in India, message our India number below.

How do I pay?

Tap "Buy now" on any bag to pay securely online by card, Apple Pay or Google Pay through Ziina, a payment provider licensed by the Central Bank of the UAE. The price includes AED 15 delivery. You can also order on WhatsApp and pay with a link we send you.

Will my bag look exactly like the photos?

Every bag is made by hand, so beadwork and colours vary slightly from piece to piece. That is part of its charm. Ask us and we'll send a photo or video of the exact bag you'll receive.

What if there's a problem with my order?

If your bag arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, message us on WhatsApp within 48 hours of delivery with a photo and we'll make it right.

How do I care for my bag?

Spot clean gently with a dry or slightly damp cloth. Do not wash or soak. Store flat, away from direct sun, and stuff totes with paper to keep their shape.

Do you take bulk or gifting orders?

Yes. We supply weddings, corporate events, festive gifting and boutiques. Message us with the quantity and date and we'll send options and a quote.
