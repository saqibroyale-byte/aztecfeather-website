OUR STORY

Crafted with roots.
Styled for now.

Aztec Feather began in India with a love for handcrafted objects, tactile fabrics and accessories full of character. We sold at exhibitions and online, and grew a community of more than 4,500 people on Instagram.

Today we bring that same joyful, bohemian spirit to Dubai, working with the same artisan suppliers, one distinctive piece at a time.
